The split

Your split

Mon Push A · Tue Pull A · Thu Push B · Fri Pull B — each muscle twice a week. Side delts on both push days for width; traps, rear delts and arms on the pull days. Each day ends with an arm pump — two triceps finishers on push days, biceps on pull days — so you look full for posing without cooking tomorrow's muscle.

How to grow while cutting
  • Progressive overload. The weights below are your current loads. Every week, add reps or a small jump (2.5 kg upper body) on presses and rows. Growth comes from the bar getting heavier over time — not the pump.
  • Eat in a deficit but keep protein high — that's what keeps the muscle on while the fat comes off. Cutting reveals the size; it doesn't build it, so keep lifting heavy.
  • Last set of isolations (raises, curls, pushdowns) — take it to the point you can't do another clean rep.
  • Machine settings. Next to the weight: height = your seat/chair height; distance = how far the seat or arms are set — the reach (e.g. how far the chest-press handles sit from you). Dial the same numbers every time so the loads compare week to week.
